Remove exactly the six entry strings knip flagged, and nothing else:

- `packages/shared.entry`: remove `"src/index.ts"` and `"scripts/seed-rosters.ts"`. That empties
  the array — delete the whole `"entry"` key for this workspace rather than leaving `"entry": []`.
- `packages/server.entry`: remove `"src/main.ts"` and `"src/index.ts"`. That empties the array —
  delete the whole `"entry"` key for this workspace.
- `packages/client.entry`: remove `"src/main.tsx"` and `"scripts/check-contrast.ts"`, **keeping**
  `"index.html"`. Result: `"entry": ["index.html"]`.

**Critical — why the empty array must become a deleted key, not `[]`:** an explicit `"entry": []`
tells knip this workspace has zero configured entry points and suppresses its default entry
resolution, which would make knip report most of the workspace as unused files. Deleting the key
lets knip fall back to its defaults plus the `package.json`-derived and plugin-derived entries
that made these patterns redundant in the first place.

Leave untouched:

- `$schema`
- `ignoreDependencies: ["husky"]`
- all three `project` arrays (none were flagged)
- `"index.html"` in the client entry (not flagged; it is what knip's Vite plugin resolves
  `src/main.tsx` through — removing it would orphan the whole client entry graph)

Expected resulting file:

- `$schema` and `ignoreDependencies` unchanged
- `packages/shared` → only a `project` key
- `packages/server` → only a `project` key
- `packages/client` → `entry: ["index.html"]` plus its unchanged `project` key

Keep the file Prettier-clean (2-space indent, trailing newline) — `lint-staged` runs
`prettier --write` on `*.json`, so a malformed file will be reformatted out from under you on commit.

**Contingency — do not force a green run.** Re-run `pnpm knip` after the edit. If removing a
pattern produces NEW findings (an `Unused files` section, or new unused exports that were not in
the baseline listed in `<planner_findings>`), that entry was load-bearing rather than redundant:
restore that one specific entry string, re-run, and report which entry had to be kept and what
finding it suppressed. Do not silently add `ignore` rules, `ignoreExportsUsedInFile`, or any other
new knip option to paper over a new finding — the goal is a genuinely clean graph, not a
suppressed one.
</action>
