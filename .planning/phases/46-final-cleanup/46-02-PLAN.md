<objective>
Fix the confirmed auto-reselect gap in the client store (CLEANUP-05, folded todo `2026-08-23-ux-no-auto-reselect-after-interrupt-prompt-resumes.md`) and collapse the duplicated phase-to-config dispatch that exists in both of the store's parallel selection trees (CLEANUP-09).

Purpose: `setGameState`'s clearing branch treats every phase transition identically, so returning from an interrupt prompt to `MOVE` drops the selection of a piece that still has pace remaining — the player has to re-find and re-click it. Separately, the phase→`ResponseMoveValidHexConfig` mapping is written twice (once as six hardcoded branch arguments in `selectPiece`, once as a five-level nested ternary in `setGameState`), which is the exact two-tree-drift shape the file's own comments blame for BUG-09.
Output: A mid-move piece survives an interrupt round-trip with its ring intact; the six response-move configs live in one exported keyed table consumed by both trees; both behaviours are pinned by tests.
</objective>
