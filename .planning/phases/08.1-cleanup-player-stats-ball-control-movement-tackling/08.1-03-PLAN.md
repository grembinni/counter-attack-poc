<objective>
Fix the three client-side UX issues that make movement frustrating and unreadable: make piece selection "sticky" so a player can chain step-moves without re-clicking (D-17/D-18/D-19), tint valid move hexes that sit in an opponent's Zone of Influence so steal/tackle risk is visible (D-20/D-21), and replace the placeholder inner-ring ball indicator with a directional white possession dot pointing toward the carrier's scoring goal (D-15/D-16).

Purpose: These are pure client rendering/state additions with no server interaction. They consume already-exported shared utilities (`getZoIDefenders`, `hexesInRange`, `validateMove`) and follow the existing Zustand/SVG patterns. Implements MOVE-05's playability surface (movement is usable end-to-end). Depends on Plan 02 so the extended shared types compile cleanly when the client imports from shared.

Output: `useGameStore.ts` setGameState refactored for sticky selection; `HexGrid.tsx` with a zoiRiskSet and ZoI overlay plus `attackingTeam` passed down; `PieceOverlay.tsx` rendering the directional possession dot; `HexGrid.module.css` with a `.hexZoIRisk` class. Verification ends with a human checkpoint for the three visual/interaction behaviors (no headless test exists for Zustand+socket UI per VALIDATION.md).
</objective>
