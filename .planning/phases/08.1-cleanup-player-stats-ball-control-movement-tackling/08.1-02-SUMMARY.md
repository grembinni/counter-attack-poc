## Summary

Completed and corrected the ball-contest mechanics: added TACKLE_ATTEMPT type+trigger, fixed die===6 auto-steal, added tackle duel resolution in applyMove, fixed ball.position tracking on every move, and refactored applyMove to receive pre-generated dice from the GAME_MOVE handler.

## What Was Built

- **types.ts**: `TACKLE_ATTEMPT` added to ActionEventType union and ActionEvent discriminant (defenderId, carrierId, defenderDie, carrierDie, defenderCombined, carrierCombined, result, timestamp). STEAL_ATTEMPT discriminant extended with `defenderDie` and `defenderCombined` fields (D-07).
- **moveValidator.ts**: TACKLE_ATTEMPT effect added to MoveResult union. Fires when a defender (different team than carrier) moves to a hex adjacent to the carrier. Mutually exclusive with STEAL_ATTEMPT (D-10).
- **moveValidator.test.ts**: 3 new TACKLE_ATTEMPT trigger tests (fires for opponent, not same team, not when no carrier — D-10).
- **gameEngine.ts**: `applyMove` refactored — optional `dice?:` 4th parameter, no internal `rollDice()` call (D-08). `die === 6 || combined >= 10` steal check (D-06). TACKLE_ATTEMPT block: defender-wins-on-tie duel, possession+ball transfer on success, defender always moves to destination (D-11). `ball.position` updated on every return path (D-13/D-14).
- **gameHandlers.ts**: GAME_MOVE handler pre-generates `stealDie`, `tackleDie`, `carrierDie` and passes to `applyMove`. All existing guards preserved (D-08, D-12).
- **gameEngine.test.ts**: New tests — ball.position tracking on carrier move, die===6 auto-steal, steal combined threshold, tackle success (possession transfer), tackle fail (defender moves, ball unchanged).
- **gameEngine.phase8.test.ts**: Updated stale STEAL_ATTEMPT fixture to include new required fields.
