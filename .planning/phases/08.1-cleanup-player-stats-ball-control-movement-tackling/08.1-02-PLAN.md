<objective>
Complete and correct the ball-contest mechanics in the move pipeline: fix the steal check to include the die===6 auto-steal, add the new TACKLE_ATTEMPT mechanic (defender moves adjacent to carrier → dribbling-vs-tackling duel), fix the ball.position bug so the ball follows the carrier, and refactor `applyMove` to receive pre-generated dice from the GAME_MOVE handler (removing the internal `rollDice()` call that violates the pure-engine contract).

Purpose: The current `applyMove` (a) only checks `combined >= 10` and misses the rulebook die===6 auto-steal (D-06), (b) has no tackle mechanic at all (D-09..D-12), (c) never updates `ball.position` when the carrier walks (D-13/D-14), and (d) rolls dice internally (D-08, Pitfall 1). These are interdependent — all four are resolved by editing the same `applyMove` return paths once. Implements MOVE-04 (steal/tackle triggers) and MOVE-05 (successful steal/tackle ends movement and transfers possession).

Output: `types.ts` and `moveValidator.ts` extended with TACKLE_ATTEMPT; `gameEngine.ts` applyMove refactored and corrected; `gameHandlers.ts` GAME_MOVE handler pre-generating dice; new/updated unit tests covering die===6 steal, tackle duel both outcomes, and ball.position tracking.
</objective>
