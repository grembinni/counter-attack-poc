<objective>
Fix BUG-30: post-game replay does not reconstruct all player positions at kickoff after a goal is scored — some pieces retain stale pre-goal positions. Add a `piecesAfter` field to the `GOAL` `ActionEvent` carrying the full post-reset piece array, populate it at both goal-scored construction sites, and apply it in `buildReplayFrames` so the replay frame rebuilds every piece to its kickoff formation position (D-01). Verify the second-half kickoff reset path is also correct in replay (D-02). Fold in the same-defect-class GK_KICK/LOOSE_BALL_LAND replay-visibility todo as a verify-then-fix-if-needed task.

Purpose: A goal is the single most-important replay moment; showing half the team teleporting to stale positions breaks the replay's credibility. This is the canonical replay-reconstruction defect class.
Output: `piecesAfter` on the `GOAL` event, both construction sites and `buildReplayFrames` updated, and regression tests in `replay.integration.test.ts`.
</objective>
