  <name>Task 4: Live two-browser verification of the decline flow</name>

  <what-built>
    A game-creation toggle (Tackle/Steal Decline Prompt, checked by default) that inserts an
    Attempt/Decline prompt before every tackle or steal duel. With two or more eligible defenders
    the defending manager is prompted one at a time in tackling order, highest first. Declining
    does not count as an attempt and leaves the amber risk ring live so the same defender can be
    prompted again on a later move step. A failed attempt moves on to the next eligible defender;
    a successful one ends the sequence. With the toggle off, duels resolve immediately exactly as
    before.
  </what-built>

  <how-to-verify>
    1. Start the app (`pnpm dev` at the repo root, or the server and client dev scripts) and open
       two browser windows against it.
    2. Create a room in window A. On the settings screen, confirm the new
       "Tackle/Steal Decline Prompt" checkbox is present and CHECKED by default. Leave it checked
       and confirm settings. Join from window B and play through to a Movement Phase.
    3. Move the ball carrier onto a hex inside an opposing defender's Zone of Influence (an amber
       risk ring hex).
    4. Confirm the defending manager's window shows the "Challenge for the Ball?" panel with
       Attempt and Decline buttons naming the defender and the carrier, and the attacking
       manager's window shows the waiting message with no buttons.
    5. Click Decline. Confirm play resumes to the same Movement Phase slot, the match log shows a
       "declined to challenge" entry in BOTH windows, and re-selecting the carrier still shows the
       amber risk ring for that same defender.
    6. Move the carrier one more step still inside that defender's range. Confirm the prompt
       appears again for the same defender.
    7. Click Attempt this time. Confirm the duel resolves (dice result appears in the log) and the
       result is what you would expect from the pre-toggle behaviour.
    8. Set up a position where TWO defenders are in range of one destination hex, with different
       tackling stats. Move the carrier there and confirm the HIGHER-tackling defender is prompted
       first, then — after declining or after a failed attempt — the second one is prompted.
    9. Confirm a successful steal or tackle ends the sequence immediately, without prompting any
       remaining defender.
    10. Move the carrier out of range and confirm the ring and prompt no longer appear for that
        defender.
    11. Start a fresh game with the toggle UNCHECKED. Confirm a tackle/steal duel resolves
        immediately with no prompt panel, exactly as before this phase.
  </how-to-verify>
