<objective>
Adapt the existing `LineupAssignmentScreen` (D-01: same screen, same drag-and-drop
mechanics as the pre-match lineup step) to also operate on live `GameState` mid-match, as
an additive `mode: 'pregame' | 'midmatch'` branch. This plan builds the component; plan
40-06 mounts it in a modal behind the persistent SUB button.

Purpose: SUB-02 requires drag-and-drop substitution on the roster screen and SUB-07
requires a clear "unavailable" indicator; D-01 forbids building a parallel roster UI.
Every existing pre-match call site must keep working untouched — `mode` defaults to
`'pregame'`.

Output: a mid-match-capable roster screen plus the client-side test coverage the
VALIDATION map assigns to SUB-07.
</objective>
