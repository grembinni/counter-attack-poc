<objective>
Carry the pre-match bench into live match state, make the roster survive every formation
reset, and make a red card visible on the bench (D-13).

Purpose: D-01/D-02 carry the pre-match lineup/bench split forward into live `GameState`
for the first time — today `benchIds` is discarded at `LINEUP_CONFIRM`. Separately,
`buildKickOffPieces` rebuilds all 22 pieces from the DEFAULT squad order after every goal
and at half-time, which would resurrect a substituted-out player (breaking SUB-03/SUB-07)
and wipe `redCarded` (making D-08's permanent headcount cap evaporate). Both are
prerequisites for substitutions being real rather than cosmetic.

D-13 adds the third piece: Phase 39's shipped booking code dismisses a red-carded player
from the pitch (`onPitch: false`) and leaves no trace on any roster surface. This plan
appends a `status: 'redCarded'` bench entry at that exact site, so the substitution screen
shows WHO is unavailable and WHY. The piece itself deliberately stays in `state.pieces` —
`maxOnPitchFor`'s `11 - redCardCount` math (D-08/D-09) counts red cards from `pieces` and
is explicitly unchanged by D-13.

Scope note (D-10 retracted): this plan generates NO bench players. There is no free-agent
pool draft, no auto-fill and no seeding of any kind. A Standard-mode room reaches kick-off
with an empty bench and that is correct (D-12).

Output: seeded bench state, the D-13 red-card bench relocation, and a continuity overlay at
the four in-engine reset sites.
Plan 40-05 applies the same overlay to the three reset sites that live in `gameHandlers.ts`.
</objective>
