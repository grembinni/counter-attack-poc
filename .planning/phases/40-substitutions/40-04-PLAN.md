<objective>
Give every live match a usable bench — including Standard-mode rooms, whose 11-player
squads would otherwise leave substitutions a no-op — and make the roster survive every
formation reset.

Purpose: D-01/D-02 carry the pre-match lineup/bench split forward into live `GameState`
for the first time — today `benchIds` is discarded at `LINEUP_CONFIRM`. Separately,
`buildKickOffPieces` rebuilds all 22 pieces from the DEFAULT squad order after every goal
and at half-time, which would resurrect a substituted-out player (breaking SUB-03/SUB-07)
and wipe `redCarded` (making D-08's permanent headcount cap evaporate). Both are
prerequisites for substitutions being real rather than cosmetic.

D-10 closes the Standard-mode gap: when a team's derived bench is empty at kick-off, the
server drafts 4 free agents (one per GK/DEF/MID/FWD) onto that bench. This is a
server-only decision made inside `buildInitialGameState` — no client input, no new
pre-match step (D-02 still forbids a matchday-squad screen).

Output: seeded bench state (incl. the D-10 auto-fill) and a continuity overlay at the
four in-engine reset sites.
Plan 40-05 applies the same overlay to the three reset sites that live in `gameHandlers.ts`.
</objective>
