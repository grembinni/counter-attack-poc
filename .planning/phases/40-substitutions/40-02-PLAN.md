| T-40-06   | Tampering              | Substitution as a budget-refresh exploit | mitigate    | The substitute inherits the slot id and therefore all per-cycle bookkeeping (`movedPieceIds`, `paceUsedByPieceId`, corner/goal-kick per-piece pace maps); no tracking field is scrubbed, so a sub cannot restore a spent movement or reposition allowance. Asserted by test. |

Asserted by test.    |
