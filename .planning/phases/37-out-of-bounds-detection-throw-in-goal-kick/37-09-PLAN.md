<objective>
Complete the server side of the dedicated Goal Kick flow: target selection against a teammate's head, the 1-player-per-team 3-hex travel-movement window, and the accuracy resolution that routes an accurate kick into a mandatory header and an inaccurate kick into a Loose Ball (GOALKICK-04, GOALKICK-05, D-01, D-02).

Purpose: Plan 37-08 stopped at `applyGoalKickChoice` — its `'kick'` branch parks the state in `GOAL_KICK_TARGET` with `goalKickTeam` and `goalKickGkId` populated and nothing to consume them. This plan builds the two remaining engine functions and the three handler wirings that carry the state through `GOAL_KICK_TARGET` and `GOAL_KICK_MOVE` to a real delivery. Per D-02 it adds zero new dice or scoring math: the accuracy check is `computeCombinedScore`, the accurate-delivery branch is the existing `HIGH_PASS` to `HEADER` shape, and the inaccurate branch is the existing `LOOSE_BALL` drop — which is already out-of-bounds-aware because Plan 37-04 hooked that site.

Output: `applyGoalKickTarget`, `applyGoalKickMoveEnd`, the `GAME_GOAL_KICK_TARGET` handler, the `GOAL_KICK_MOVE` branches of `GAME_MOVE` and `GAME_END_TURN`, and the Wave-0 `goalKick.integration.test.ts`.

## Decisions locked by this plan

- **GOALKICK-05's "must target a teammate's head" is enforced at target-selection time.** `applyGoalKickTarget` rejects any target hex not currently occupied by a piece of `state.goalKickTeam` other than the goalkeeper. This is the literal reading of the requirement and it is the only rule that makes the mandatory-header outcome reachable by construction rather than by luck.
- **No opponent-final-third restriction.** The existing `applyGKKickTarget` forbids punting into the opponent's final third, but that is a GK-punt rule with no basis in GOALKICK-01..06. Per D-01 the goal kick is structurally independent, so this plan does not import that restriction. The teammate-occupancy rule is the binding constraint. Record this in the SUMMARY so Phase 38 does not "restore" it by analogy.
- **No distance cap on the kick.** GOALKICK-03/04/05 specify an accuracy check, not a range. The `GK_PUNT` analog is likewise uncapped. `validatePass` is deliberately not called (it would add path-blocking and interception collection, neither of which applies to a ball kicked over heads).
- **`ballAfter.carrierId` is `null` on both resolution branches.** This differs from the `GK_KICK` analog, where an accurate punt is caught by the receiver. An accurate goal kick lands in a header contest, so nobody carries the ball. Document the divergence inline.
- **Accuracy uses `computeCombinedScore(gk.highPass, kickDie, [])`, not the inline `kickDie + gk.highPass` the `GK_KICK` handler uses.** D-02 and the RESEARCH Don't-Hand-Roll table both require the shared function; the inline form silently skips the DICE-04 penalty clamp.
