## Decisions locked by this plan

- **GOALKICK-02 eligibility (literal reading of "both final-thirds' players"):** eligible pieces are **every piece of either team whose position at trigger time is in `homeThird` or `awayThird`** (equivalently `computeBallZone(piece.position) !== 'middle'`), partitioned into `gkTeam` and `opponent` lists. This is the literal reading of the requirement text; RESEARCH.md's narrower "each team's own final third" reading is not adopted. Eligibility is computed once, at trigger time, and never recomputed — mirroring `freeMoveEligibleIds`' precompute-both-teams-at-trigger-time contract, so a piece that walks out of a final third mid-window keeps its remaining budget and a piece that walks in does not gain one.
- **Budget model:** per-piece, 6 hexes each, tracked in `goalKickUsedPace` keyed by piece id — the `freeMoveUsedPace` shape. NOT the `FREE_KICK_STAGES` distinct-piece-count model. Movement is single-hex-per-click with adjacency and occupancy checks, exactly like `applyFreeMove`.
- **Window ordering:** `GOAL_KICK_SETUP_GK` (goalkeeper's team) always runs first, then `GOAL_KICK_SETUP_OPPONENT` — GOALKICK-02's explicit ordering. A window with an empty eligible list is skipped rather than presenting a dead panel.
- **No reuse of `applyGKRestart` / `applyGKKickTarget` / `applyGKKickMove` (D-01):** the new functions must not call them. Copying their structure is required; calling them is forbidden. A grep assertion enforces this.
