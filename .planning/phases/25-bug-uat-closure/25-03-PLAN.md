<objective>
Implement the two UX-15 interaction changes (D-19, D-20) that are isolated to EventBanner and ActionPanel. D-20: replace the HEADER "Continue" push-button for pass-accuracy with an auto-dismissing EventBanner popup ("Accurate Pass!" / "Loose Ball!") plus an auto-fire of `emitHeaderAccuracyAck()` after 1500ms on the attacking player's client only. Purpose: Remove an unnecessary click from match flow and align the move counter with the physical board game's "activation" feel, both surfaced in v1.3 playtesting.
Output: HP_ACCURACY banner case with a 1500ms variant, ActionPanel auto-advance effect replacing the button, and a selected-piece decrement in the `remaining` derivation.
</objective>
