<objective>
Find the middle ground for jersey number vertical centering. Plan 25-02 changed `dominantBaseline="central"` to `dominantBaseline="middle"` to fix the "too low" original placement (D-17), but the UAT (Plan 05 Task 3, item 4) confirmed that `"middle"` is too high — the numbers appear slightly above center.

The strategy: revert to `dominantBaseline="central"` and add a small upward `dy` offset (`dy="-0.5"`) to nudge the text to the visual midpoint. `"central"` aligns the mathematical center of the em square to the `y` coordinate (tends to look slightly low on-screen); `"middle"` aligns the midpoint of the em box (tends to look slightly high). A `dy="-0.5"` shift (half a user-space pixel upward) from the `"central"` baseline is the standard correction for this SVG font metric discrepancy.

If `dy="-0.5"` is insufficient (still appears low), try `dy="-1"`. Verify visually at R=12 (gameplay) and R=30 (selection screen) before committing.

Purpose: Correct the overshot Plan 25-02 centering fix — find the stable midpoint between "too low" and "too high".
Output: Single attribute change + dy addition on the jersey number `<text>` element in PieceOverlay.tsx.
</objective>

    ```tsx

      x={cx}
      y={cy}
      dy="-0.5"
      textAnchor="middle"
      dominantBaseline="central"
      fontSize={15}
      fontWeight={700}
      fill={numberColor}
      fontStyle={piece.role === 'GK' ? 'italic' : 'normal'}
      pointerEvents="none"
    >
      {playerNumber}
    </text>
    ```
