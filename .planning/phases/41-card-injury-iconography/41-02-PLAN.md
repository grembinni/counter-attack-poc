<objective>
Close the bench data gap that blocks ICON-03: `BenchEntry` today carries only
`playerId`/`jerseyNumber`/`status`, so a subbed-off or sent-off player's yellow card and injury
history is dropped the moment they leave the pitch and the bench card has nothing to render.
Add two optional fields and populate them at the two existing construction sites.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 41 Success Criterion 2 requires a bench player "booked **or** injured" to show
that status. `41-UI-SPEC.md` locks this as RESEARCH.md Option 1 (extend `BenchEntry`), explicitly
overriding the research's cautious red-card-only default.
Output: two additive optional fields, two one-line-each engine copy-throughs, and server tests.

This is the phase's ONLY non-client change. It is data propagation of already-validated values,
not new game logic and not a new input surface.
</objective>
