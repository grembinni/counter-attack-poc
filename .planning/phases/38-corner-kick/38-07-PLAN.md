<objective>
Build the Corner Kick presentation layer: one `CornerKickSetupPanel` branching internally across
all five corner phases (mirroring how `GoalKickSetupPanel` covers Goal Kick's five), plus the
non-compiler-enforced registration sites — `GameBoard`'s `PHASE_LABEL` and phase-dispatch ternary,
`BallLocationRing`'s `BALL_MARKER_PHASES`, and `ActionLog`'s event rendering.

TypeScript will NOT catch (STATE.md's documented recurring bug class). A missed entry ships as a
silently panel-less or marker-less phase.

Output: a fully navigable corner-kick UI matching the 38-UI-SPEC copywriting and styling contract.
</objective>
