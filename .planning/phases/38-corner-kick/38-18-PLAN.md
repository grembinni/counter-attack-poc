<objective>
Close the client half of 38-15 defects 1 and 2: stop enforcing and stop advertising the 6-hex
reposition budget, and render a piece that has already been repositioned this window with the
existing `activated` treatment so a manager can see at a glance which of their six players are
spent.

Purpose: the server half (38-17) removes the cap and adds the activation ledger, but the client
independently gates selectability on `cornerKickUsedPace < 6` in two places and prints
"up to 6 hexes each" in the panel. Left unchanged, the client would silently keep the bug the human
verifier reported even after the server is fixed.

Output: uncapped corner reposition selection, the orange-ring/red-X activated treatment wired to
the new ledger, corrected panel copy, and a player-facing sentence for the new `PIECE_LOCKED` code.
</objective>
