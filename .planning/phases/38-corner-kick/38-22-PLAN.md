<objective>
Make the clear-out step playable and legible in the browser, and stop offering the defending side
any destination inside the 3-hex corner exclusion zone anywhere in the corner sequence.

Purpose: 38-20 and 38-21 make the clear-out authoritative and reachable over the socket, but with
no client branch the phase would render with no panel, no selectable pieces, and no ball marker —
the game would appear frozen. The exclusion zone likewise needs a client mirror, or defenders will
repeatedly click hexes the server rejects.

Output: the clear-out panel branch, the clear-out selection and destination logic, exclusion-zone
filtering for every defending destination set, and the three registration-list entries the new
phase needs on the client.
</objective>
