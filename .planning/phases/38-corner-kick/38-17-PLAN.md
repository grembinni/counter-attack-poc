<objective>
Close 38-15 defects 1 and 2 on the server: make CORNER-03 reposition movement free/uncapped, and
replace the removed hex budget with the rule that actually limits the window — each piece may be
activated once per window, which is what makes CORNER-03's "up to 6 players" mean six DISTINCT
players per side.

the window is limited by a 6-hex-per-piece budget that the rulebook does not have; with that budget
removed, nothing would stop a manager repositioning the same two players unlimited distances in all
three of their rounds. The activation ledger is the rulebook's actual constraint.

Output: `applyCornerKickReposition` with no pace cap and a persistent per-window activation lock,
plus the matching clear at the pre-kick boundary and the matching refund in `applyUndo`.

## Interpretation recorded as D-GAP-03 (needs the human's explicit ruling at the 38-24 checkpoint)

`38-15-SUMMARY.md` defect 2 says a piece "should be flagged activated during the '2-2-2' free-move
stages, and that activation should reset for the final pre-kick move of up to 3 hexes", and
explicitly says the exact mechanic needs clarification. The interpretation this plan implements,
and the evidence for it:

1. `REQUIREMENTS.md` CORNER-03 reads "repositions up to 6 players, alternating 2 players at a
   time" — six DISTINCT players, which the current per-stage-only cap does not enforce.
2. Defect 1 removes the 6-hex budget. With no budget and no permanent lock, "up to 6 players"
   would be unenforceable — a manager could move the same two players unlimited distances three
   times each.
3. The codebase already has exactly this mechanism: `FREE_KICK_SETUP` pairs a per-stage
   `freeKickPlacedPieceIds` with a permanent `movedPieceIds` lock, and `HexGrid.tsx`'s `isSpentNow`
   already renders that pair as the `'activated'` orange-ring + red-X state. `CORNER_KICK_STAGES`'s
   own JSDoc in `offside.ts` explicitly documents corner's divergence from that permanent lock —
   this plan reverts the divergence.
4. "Reset for the final pre-kick move" maps exactly to clearing the ledger at the
   `CORNER_KICK_REPOSITION` to `CORNER_KICK_FINAL_SETUP` boundary.

This is recorded as **D-GAP-03** and MUST be presented to the human verifier for an explicit
ruling in plan 38-24, exactly as 38-14 did for D-GAP-02.
