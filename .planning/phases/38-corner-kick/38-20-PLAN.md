## Design decisions this plan locks (record them in the SUMMARY)

- **One phase value plus a slot field**, not a second `_ATTACKING`/`_DEFENDING` phase pair. The
  clear-out reuses `cornerKickClearOutSlot` exactly the way `CORNER_KICK_FINAL_SETUP` reuses
  `cornerKickMoveSlot`, halving the new registration surface.
- **The clear-out is not undoable.** `CORNER_KICK_GK_SETUP_ATTACKING`,
  `CORNER_KICK_GK_SETUP_DEFENDING`, and `CORNER_KICK_TAKER_SELECT` are all deliberately absent from
  `validUndoPhases` (see the comment at `gameHandlers.ts:1555`); the clear-out joins them. The
  movement constraints below are tight enough that there is nothing meaningful to undo.
- **Only pieces currently inside the zone may move.** This is what bounds the step: a piece that
  has cleared the zone is done, so a manager cannot walk pieces arbitrarily up the pitch under
  cover of the clear-out.
- **The exclusion zone is NOT enforced on intermediate clear-out steps.** A defender starting at
  distance 1 is inside the zone by definition and its first step is still inside it. Enforcing the
  zone there would make the clear-out impossible. The zone is enforced by the confirm gate and by
  the three later-phase guards.
- **The step rule lives in shared, not here.** `isLegalClearOutStep` (38-16) is the single source
  of truth so the client's destination highlighting in 38-22 cannot disagree with the server.
