<objective>
Mirror plan 38-27's server change on the client: turn `CORNER_KICK_REPOSITION` into a bounded-area
single-destination placement, show the activated marker only once that placement is complete, and
stop showing an activated marker at all during the `CORNER_KICK_FINAL_SETUP` pre-kick window.

Purpose: `38-24-SUMMARY.md` bug 2 ("single hex selection within an allowed area like on a free kick.
Player should be marked activated after the move") and bug 3 ("three step move immediately marks
player as activated - is not needed for the movement"). Today `selectPiece` calls
`computeFreeMoveValidHexes`, which returns only the six adjacent hexes, so a reposition is an
unbounded sequence of one-hex clicks; and `HexGrid.tsx`'s `isSpentNow` paints the pre-kick piece
`activated` after its FIRST of three hexes.

Output: a client that matches the rewritten `applyCornerKickReposition` exactly, and a pre-kick
window with no activation marker.
</objective>

## Locked assumptions

This plan inherits plan 38-27's two locked assumptions verbatim and must not diverge from them:

- **A-GAP3-BUDGET** — `CORNER_KICK_STAGES` keeps `max: 2` per stage and `cornerKickActivatedIds`
  keeps locking a piece out for the rest of the window; six distinct pieces per side across three
  stages, unchanged.
- **A-GAP3-AREA** — attacking side may be placed on any unoccupied on-pitch hex; defending side on
  any unoccupied on-pitch hex outside `isWithinCornerExclusionZone(hex, cornerKickHex)`.

The client's destination set MUST be exactly the set the server accepts. Any divergence produces a
hex that looks clickable and is then rejected, which is the class of defect this phase has already
shipped twice.
