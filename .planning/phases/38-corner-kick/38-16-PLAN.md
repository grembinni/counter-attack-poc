       destination-hex computation (38-22), so the two can never disagree. Returns true when
       `hexDistance(to, cornerHex)` is strictly greater than `hexDistance(from, cornerHex)` AND
       `hexDistance(to, goalHex)` is less than or equal to `hexDistance(from, goalHex)`.
       Document both halves: the strict away-from-corner term is what guarantees the clear-out
       terminates; the goal term is "less than or equal" rather than "strictly less" because a
       piece already level with the goal-mouth row would otherwise have no legal step at all, and
       "not retreating from goal" is the faithful reading of the rule. Document explicitly that
       this function checks GEOMETRY ONLY — adjacency, pitch membership, and occupancy remain the

    5. `isSpillCornerDirection(direction: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6, keeperTeamId: 'home' | 'away'): boolean`

       vertical direction (`qStep === 0`) is "next to" the keeper, a byline-ward direction is
       "behind", and only the two directions with a column step toward the opposite goal are
