## The rule, restated exactly as ruled

> If a goalkeeper saves a shot, roll a dice. If your roll is equal to or higher than the
> goalkeeper's Handling attribute, run a "Loose ball": if Direction is next or behind the GK it is
> a Corner Kick. If the direction is in front of GK, roll for distance, and continue play normally.

`isSpillCornerDirection` (38-16) encodes "next to or behind" as: the direction's column step is
zero (purely vertical, i.e. alongside the keeper) or points toward the keeper's own byline. Only
the two directions stepping toward the opposite goal are "in front".

**Why a dedicated marker rather than `ball.lastTouchedBy` alone:** the SHOT duel-tie branch at
`gameEngine.ts` ~2793 ALSO sets `ball.lastTouchedBy` to the goalkeeper and ALSO transitions to
`LOOSE_BALL`. Gating on "last toucher is a goalkeeper" would silently apply the spill rule to a
duel-tie deflection, which the rulebook text does not cover. `gkSpillKeeperId` (38-16) is set only
by the spill branch, so it is exactly the gate the ruling describes and nothing more.
