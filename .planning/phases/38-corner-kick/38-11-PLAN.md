<objective>
Give the two corner-kick reposition windows a working Undo affordance, so the server-side Undo
fixed by 38-10 is actually reachable by a human.

Purpose: this is a blocking enabler for gap-closure item 7. `gameHandlers.ts` lists
`CORNER_KICK_REPOSITION` and `CORNER_KICK_FINAL_SETUP` in `validUndoPhases`, and 38-09-PLAN.md's
step 8 requires a live "press Undo during the alternating window" check — but `GameBoard.tsx`
(lines 360-370) routes all five corner phases to `CornerKickSetupPanel`, which renders no Undo
button, and `ActionPanel` (the only other component with one, besides `FreeKickSetupPanel`) is
never mounted during those phases. Undo is therefore unreachable from the UI today, and item 7's
walkthrough cannot be performed without this.

Output: two `Undo` buttons in `CornerKickSetupPanel` with a client-side enable/disable mirror of
`applyUndo`'s boundary logic, modelled on `FreeKickSetupPanel.tsx`'s existing `canUndo` + Undo
button pair.
</objective>
