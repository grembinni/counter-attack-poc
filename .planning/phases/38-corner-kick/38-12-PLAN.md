<objective>
Close 38-REVIEW.md WR-01 and WR-02, the two non-blocking correctness defects left after the Undo
fixes: post-match replay shows stale piece positions after any corner-kick goalkeeper placement
or pre-kick reposition, and `applyCornerKickReposition` hardcodes the per-stage distinct-piece
cap instead of reading its documented single source of truth.

Purpose: gap-closure items 5 and 6 from `38-09-SUMMARY.md`. WR-01 is a real display-correctness
bug in the shipped replay feature — a keeper who repositioned for a corner teleports back to a
stale hex for the rest of the stream. WR-02 is latent divergence risk: `CORNER_KICK_STAGES` is
documented as the single source of truth and both `CornerKickSetupPanel.tsx` and
`useGameStore.ts` already read `max` from it, while the engine's own enforcement path does not.

Output: two new `moveGroup` accumulation arms in `buildReplayFrames`, a `CORNER_KICK_STAGES`
table read in `applyCornerKickReposition`, and tests that fail if either regresses.
</objective>
