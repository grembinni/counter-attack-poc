  <what-built>
    Six gap-closure plans landed since the deferred checkpoint:

    - **38-10** — `applyUndo` now handles both corner reposition phases. During
      `CORNER_KICK_FINAL_SETUP` it finds and reverses the `CORNER_KICK_MOVE` event (it previously
      always failed with `NOTHING_TO_UNDO`). During `CORNER_KICK_REPOSITION` it now refunds the
      piece's 6-hex budget and, per D-GAP-01, drops the piece from that stage's 2-distinct-piece
      cap once its only move in that stage is undone.
    - **38-11** — `CornerKickSetupPanel` now renders an `Undo` button in both reposition windows.
      There was previously no way to press Undo at all in those phases: `GameBoard` routes them to
      the corner panel, and only `ActionPanel` and `FreeKickSetupPanel` had Undo buttons.
    - **38-12** — `buildReplayFrames` now tracks piece positions for `CORNER_KICK_GK_PLACE` and
      `CORNER_KICK_MOVE`, so a keeper or player who moved during a corner no longer teleports back
      to a stale hex for the rest of the replay. The engine's per-stage distinct-piece cap now
      reads `CORNER_KICK_STAGES` instead of a hardcoded 2.
    - **38-13** — **Assumption A2 corrected.** A **Low Pass** corner IS now interceptable (both the
      auto-intercept on an occupied target hex and the ZoI roll). A **High Pass** corner is still
      never interceptable and still forces the header contest. An intercepted corner now tears down
      all `cornerKick*` state so the next ordinary pass is not accuracy-gated.
    - **38-14** — The Phase 17.1 D-07 placeholder is gone. A goalkeeper who fails the handling
      check on a save now spills the ball into a real Loose Ball at their dive-adjusted hex instead
      of receiving clean possession. A scatter that crosses the keeper's own byline is classified as
      a Corner Kick to the attacking team through the same `classifyOutOfBounds` /
      `triggerOutOfBoundsRestart` path OOB-03 uses.
    - **D-GAP-02 is deliberately unresolved** and needs your ruling in step 8 below.

  <how-to-verify>
    Start the dev servers and open two browser windows joined to the same room, one per team.
    Create the game with the "Out-of-Bounds / Restarts" toggle ON.

    **1. Award the corner (OOB-03).** Play until a defending player last touches a ball that then
    crosses their own byline. Expect: the action log reads a `Corner Kick` restart, the ball marker
    sits on the corner hex, the panel heading reads `Corner Kick`, and the corner is awarded to the
    ATTACKING team.

    **2. Corner-taker (CORNER-02).** The kicking manager sees
    `Choose a player to take the corner kick.` Click an own player and Confirm. Expect that player
    and the ball to jump to the gold-ringed corner hex. An opponent's player must not be clickable.

    **3. Pre-kick window (CORNER-06).** Expect `Reposition 1 player — up to 3 hexes.`, attacking
    manager first, one player each.

    **4. Take the corner — A2 RE-CHECK (CORNER-04/05).** Expect two co-equal buttons,
    `High Pass` and `Low Pass`.
    - Take a **High Pass** into the penalty area from long range with a defender standing adjacent
      to the taker. Expect: NO interception roll, and an accurate one starts a HEADER contest.
    - Replay and take a **Low Pass** along a line that passes a defender, or straight at a
      defender-occupied hex. Expect: the target hexes at risk show the orange interception tint,
      and taking it DOES produce an interception attempt in the log. An accurate uncontested Low
      Pass delivers the ball with NO header.
    - Verify an inaccurate corner of either type still produces a Loose Ball.
    - After an intercepted Low corner, take an ordinary Standard Pass on the next possession and
      confirm it is NOT accuracy-checked (no 8+ roll appears in the log).

    **5. Visual check.** Confirm the corner panels still look like the Goal Kick / Free Kick panels:
    same `Confirm` verb, green-when-ready / orange-when-pending CTA, no container border, 11px
    constraint rows, and no new hex tint colours. The new `Undo` button should sit above `Confirm`
    and look like Free Kick's Undo.

    **6. Alternating window sanity (CORNER-03).** In the 6-hex window, confirm a third player
    becomes unselectable within one round, and that a player who moved 2 hexes in round 1 can still
    move 4 more later but not a 7th.

    **7. UNDO CHECK — the core of this re-verification.**
    - **During `CORNER_KICK_REPOSITION`:** move a player 2 hexes, then press `Undo` twice. Expect:
      the piece walks back one hex per press with no error banner, and after the second press the
      player is fully back at their starting hex AND still has their full 6-hex budget (move them 6
      hexes to confirm). Then, with two distinct players already moved this round, undo one of them
      completely and confirm a THIRD, different player becomes selectable again — this is D-GAP-01
      and needs your explicit ruling: is refunding the stage's distinct-piece slot correct, or
      should an undone piece still count against the round's 2-player cap?
    - Confirm `Undo` never reaches back into the previous manager's completed round (the button
      should go disabled at the start of your round).
    - **During `CORNER_KICK_FINAL_SETUP`:** move your one player, press `Undo`. Expect the piece to
      revert and the window to let you pick a DIFFERENT player afterwards. This path was a complete
      no-op before gap closure.

    **8. GK SAVE-SPILL CHECK — new mechanic, and one open rules question.**
    Get a shot on target that the keeper SAVES but fails the handling check on (the log will show a
    `SHOT_ATTEMPT` with outcome `LOOSE_BALL` and a handling die at or above the keeper's Handling).
    Expect: the keeper does NOT get the ball; the phase becomes a Loose Ball scattering from the
    keeper's hex, and you roll direction and distance as normal.
    - Confirm that a scatter which crosses the keeper's own goal line awards a **Corner Kick** to
      the attacking team.
    - Confirm that a scatter which stays on the pitch just continues play.
    - **D-GAP-02 RULING NEEDED:** as implemented, the spill only becomes a corner when the scatter
      actually walks off the pitch. If the keeper is standing several hexes in front of their own
      goal line, a short scatter "behind" them lands in play instead of awarding a corner. Your
      original rule text was "if the landing direction is next to or behind the GK, it is a Corner
      Kick; if in front of the GK, roll for distance and continue play". Please state which is
      correct: (a) the scatter-walk behaviour as implemented, or (b) a direction-only rule where any
      byline-ward direction awards a corner immediately regardless of the keeper's distance from
      the line.

    **9. Replay check (WR-01).** Play to full time and watch the replay. Confirm that a goalkeeper
    who repositioned for a corner, and a player who moved during the pre-kick window, both appear at
    their real post-move positions for the remainder of the replay rather than snapping back to
    where they were before the corner.
