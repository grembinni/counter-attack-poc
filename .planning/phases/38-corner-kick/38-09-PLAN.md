<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Two-browser corner-kick walkthrough and rulebook-assumption confirmation</name>
  <what-built>
    The complete Corner Kick restart (OOB-03, CORNER-01..06):

    - A ball crossing a byline after a defending touch now awards a corner to the attacking team
      instead of dead-ending (`triggerOutOfBoundsRestart`'s Phase-38 hook is replaced).
    - Five new game phases: attacking-GK reposition, defending-GK reposition, corner-taker
      selection, the alternating 6-hex window, and the pre-kick 3-hex window.
    - A new `CornerKickSetupPanel` drives all five, plus the High Pass / Low Pass choice.
    - Both corner options roll the existing 8+ combined-score accuracy check; High forces a
      header, Low does not; a High corner aimed inside the box being attacked has no distance
      limit, and is capped at 15 hexes elsewhere.
    - Full unit and socket-level integration coverage is green across all three packages.

    Three rulebook readings were implemented as recommended defaults and are marked in source with
    `Assumption A1`, `Assumption A2` and `Assumption A4` comments:
    - **A1** — the goalkeeper reposition (CORNER-01) is UNCAPPED: the keeper may be placed on any
      legal empty hex in one click. CORNER-01 states no hex cap where CORNER-03 ("up to 6 hexes")
      and CORNER-06 ("up to 3 hexes") both do.
    - **A2** — neither corner option is interceptable; both fly over adjacent defenders like the
      existing High Pass / Long Ball.
    - **A4** — both goalkeepers and the chosen corner-taker are excluded from the CORNER-03 and
      CORNER-06 repositioning windows.

  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Start the dev servers and open two browser windows joined to the same room, one per team.

    **1. Award the corner (OOB-03).** Create a game with the "Out-of-Bounds / Restarts" toggle ON.
    Play until a defending player last touches a ball that then crosses their own byline (a
    deflected shot or a loose ball works). Expect: the action log reads a `Corner Kick` restart,
    the ball marker sits on the corner hex, and the panel heading reads `Corner Kick`.
    Confirm the corner was awarded to the ATTACKING team, not the defending one.

    **2. Goalkeeper windows (CORNER-01).** Expect the ATTACKING manager to be prompted first, with
    `Goalkeeper: choose a new position, then Confirm.`, while the other window shows
    `... team is repositioning…`. Click the goalkeeper, click a destination, then Confirm; the
    defending manager is prompted next.
    → **A1 CHECK:** you should be able to place the keeper on ANY empty pitch hex, however far.
    Confirm against the physical rulebook whether an unlimited goalkeeper reposition is correct, or
    whether a cap (and which one) applies.

    **3. Corner-taker (CORNER-02).** The kicking manager sees `Choose a player to take the corner kick.`
    Click any own player and Confirm. Expect that player and the ball to jump to the corner hex
    (the hex ringed in gold). Try selecting an opponent's player — it should not be clickable.

    **4. Alternating window (CORNER-03).** Expect the ATTACKING manager to go first, able to move
    up to 2 players, each up to 6 hexes, then Confirm; then the defending manager; repeating for 3
    rounds each. Verify:
    - a third player becomes unselectable within one round;
    - a player who moved 2 hexes in round 1 can still move 4 more later, but not a 7th;
    - Confirming with players still eligible pops the "N players left to reposition, are you sure?"
      dialog, and Confirming with none left does not.
    → **A4 CHECK:** neither goalkeeper nor the corner-taker should be selectable in this window.
    Confirm that matches the rulebook.

    **5. Pre-kick window (CORNER-06).** Expect `Reposition 1 player — up to 3 hexes.`, attacking
    manager first, one player each.

    **6. Take the corner (CORNER-04/05).** Expect two co-equal buttons, `High Pass` and `Low Pass`.
    - Take a **High Pass** into the penalty area from a long distance — it should be allowed, and
      an accurate one should start a HEADER contest.
    - Replay and take a **Low Pass** — an accurate one should deliver the ball with NO header.
    - Verify an inaccurate corner of either type produces a Loose Ball.
    → **A2 CHECK:** with a defender standing adjacent to the corner-taker, neither option should
      trigger an interception roll. Confirm that matches the rulebook.

    **7. Visual check.** Confirm the corner panels look like the Goal Kick / Free Kick panels:
    same `Confirm` button verb, green-when-ready / orange-when-pending CTA, no container border,
    11px constraint rows, and no new hex tint colours anywhere on the pitch.

    **8. Undo/Replay spot check.** During the alternating window, press Undo — it should revert the
    last move but never reach back into the previous manager's completed round. After the match,
    open the replay and confirm the corner appears.

  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>
    Reply with either:
    - `approved` — everything above behaved as described and all three assumptions (A1 uncapped GK
      reposition, A2 non-interceptable corner passes, A4 GK/taker excluded from the windows) are
      correct per the rulebook; or
    - a numbered list of issues, explicitly stating for each of A1/A2/A4 whether it is CONFIRMED or
      what the correct rule is instead.
  </resume-signal>
</task>
