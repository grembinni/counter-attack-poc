  <how-to-verify>
    Start the dev servers and open two browser windows joined to the same room, one per team.
    Create the game with the "Out-of-Bounds / Restarts" toggle ON.

    Give an explicit CONFIRMED or CORRECTED verdict for EVERY numbered step. `38-15-SUMMARY.md`
    explicitly warns that steps 1, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9 were never individually re-confirmed and must
    not be assumed passing — please do not skip them even if they look obviously fine.

    **1. Award the corner (OOB-03).** Play until a defending player last touches a ball that then
    crosses their own byline. Expect: a `Corner Kick!` banner, the action log reads a Corner Kick
    restart, the ball marker sits on the corner hex, the panel heading reads `Corner Kick`, and the
    corner is awarded to the ATTACKING team.

    **2. NEW — the mandatory clear-out (defect 3).** Expect the corner to open on the clear-out
    step, NOT on the goalkeeper window. The attacking manager acts first and sees
    `{N} of your players are too close to the corner — move them toward goal.`
    - Click one of your own players inside the corner area. Expect highlighted destinations that
      all move AWAY from the corner and no further from goal — no hex that moves toward the corner
      or away from goal should be offered.
    - Try to Confirm while one of your own players is still inside the 3-hex corner area. Expect a
      rejection, not a soft "are you sure" dialog.
    - Clear them out, Confirm, and expect the turn to pass to the defending manager for their own
      clear-out, then on to the goalkeeper window.
    - Judge whether "closer to goal" is being interpreted the way you meant it. The implemented
      rule is: every single step must increase the distance from the corner AND must not increase
      the distance to the goal mouth. If you meant something stricter or looser, say so.

    **3. NEW — the permanent defender exclusion zone (defect 3).** At each of the three later
    windows, try to move a DEFENDING player to a hex within 3 hexes of the corner:
    - during the defending goalkeeper's reposition,
    - during a defending round of the 2-2-2 window,
    - during the defending slot of the pre-kick window.
    Expect the hex to be un-offered and any attempt rejected with
    `Defenders cannot be positioned within three hexes of the corner.` Then confirm an ATTACKING
    player CAN still move into that area.

    **4. Goalkeepers and corner-taker (CORNER-01 / CORNER-02).** Expect the attacking goalkeeper to
    reposition first, then the defending goalkeeper, then
    `Choose a player to take the corner kick.` Click an own player and Confirm; expect that player
    and the ball to jump to the gold-ringed corner hex. An opponent's player must not be clickable.

    **5. Take the corner — A2 RE-CHECK (CORNER-04 / CORNER-05).** This is one of the steps never
    re-confirmed. Expect two co-equal buttons, `High Pass` and `Low Pass`.
    - Take a **High Pass** into the penalty area from long range with a defender adjacent to the
      taker. Expect NO interception roll, and an accurate one starts a HEADER contest.
    - Take a **Low Pass** along a line that passes a defender, or straight at a defender-occupied
      hex. Expect the orange interception tint on the at-risk hexes and an interception attempt in
      the log. An accurate uncontested Low Pass delivers the ball with NO header.
    - Verify an inaccurate corner of either type still produces a Loose Ball.
    - After an intercepted Low corner, take an ordinary Standard Pass on the next possession and
      confirm it is NOT accuracy-checked (no 8+ roll in the log).

    **6. NEW — free movement and activation in the 2-2-2 window (defects 1 and 2, D-GAP-03).**
    - Move one player MORE than 6 hexes in a single round. Expect no budget, no cap, no rejection.
      The panel should read `up to 2, unlimited distance.`
    - Move two distinct players in your round; expect a third to become unselectable.
    - Confirm, let the opponent take their round, and come back to your second round. **Expect the
      two players you moved in round 1 to now show the orange ring with the red X and to be
      unselectable, and expect two DIFFERENT players to be selectable.** Over your three rounds you
      should be able to reposition six distinct players and no more.
    - **D-GAP-03 RULING NEEDED.** Your defect report said a player should be "flagged activated
      during the 2-2-2 stages" with the activation "reset for the final pre-kick move", and asked
      for the exact mechanic to be clarified. The implemented reading is: **one reposition per
      player for the whole 2-2-2 window (so six distinct players per side), with the lock released
      when the pre-kick window opens.** Please state whether that is correct, or describe the rule
      you meant. In particular: should a player who moved in round 1 be re-movable in round 2, or
      not?

    **7. Pre-kick window and UNDO (CORNER-06, D-GAP-01).**
    - Expect `Reposition 1 player — up to 3 hexes.`, attacking manager first, one player each.
      Confirm a player who was ALREADY repositioned during the 2-2-2 window is selectable here —
      that is the activation reset.
    - **During the 2-2-2 window:** move a player two hexes, press `Undo` twice. Expect the piece to
      walk back one hex per press with no error banner, and to be fully back at its starting hex.
      Then, with two distinct players already moved this round, undo one of them completely and
      confirm a THIRD player becomes selectable again — this is D-GAP-01, which you ruled should
      stay as implemented. Confirm it still behaves that way now the hex budget is gone.
    - Confirm `Undo` never reaches back into the previous manager's completed round.
    - **During the pre-kick window:** move your one player, press `Undo`. Expect the piece to revert
      and the window to let you pick a DIFFERENT player afterwards.
    - Note: there is deliberately NO Undo during the clear-out step or the goalkeeper windows,
      matching the existing corner-setup phases. Say so if you disagree.

    **8. GK SAVE-SPILL — D-GAP-02 AS CORRECTED.** Get a shot on target that the keeper SAVES but
    fails the handling check on (the log shows a `SHOT_ATTEMPT` with outcome `LOOSE_BALL` and a
    handling die at or above the keeper's Handling).
    - Expect the keeper does NOT get the ball.
    - **Set this up with the keeper standing several hexes OFF their own goal line** — this is
      exactly the case 38-14 got wrong. Roll a direction that is next to or behind the keeper.
      Expect an IMMEDIATE Corner Kick, with no distance roll mattering, regardless of the keeper's
      distance from the line.
    - Then roll a direction in FRONT of the keeper. Expect no corner: the distance die is used and
      play continues normally.

    **9. Visual check and replay.**
    - Confirm the corner panels still look like the Goal Kick / Free Kick panels: same `Confirm`
      verb, green-when-ready / orange-when-pending CTA, no container border, 11px constraint rows,
      and no new hex tint colours. The new clear-out panel should be indistinguishable in style
      from the others.
    - Play to full time and watch the replay. Confirm a goalkeeper who repositioned for a corner, a
      player who moved during the pre-kick window, and a player who was cleared out of the corner
      area all appear at their real post-move positions for the rest of the replay rather than
      snapping back.

    **10. NEW — restart banners (defect 4).** Confirm a transient centred banner appears on entering
    each of: Throw In, Goal Kick, Corner Kick, Free Kick — same styling and timing as the existing
    turnover banner, firing once per restart rather than repeatedly.
    - **Disclosure:** the Penalty Kick banner you asked for is NOT implemented. Penalty kicks do
      not exist in the codebase yet — they are Phase 39 scope — so there is no phase to attach a
      banner to. The banner table is built so Phase 39 adds a single row, and the gap is recorded
      in `deferred-items.md`. Please confirm you are content with that deferral or say otherwise.
