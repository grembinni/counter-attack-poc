<objective>
Extend the single-source highlight table in `HexCell.tsx` to cover every real hex-tint case and re-map the traffic-light semantics (HILITE-01/02, D-01/D-02/D-03). This is the contract plan that 33-06 (HexGrid consolidation) depends on: it adds the `gk-kick-target`, `pass-target`, and `tackle-risk` tint types, recolors `safe` gold→green and `goal` red→purple, and adds an independent `ring?: 'required' | 'confirmed'` overlay prop (mirroring `PieceOverlay`'s `isOffside`/`isMovedThisStage` pattern) for the compound centre-hex and confirmed-pass rings. It also migrates the affected `HexCell.test.tsx` and the two `HexGrid.test.tsx` color constants so the suite stays green after the recolor.

Purpose: One lookup table becomes the sole owner of every mutually-exclusive hex tint, with red reserved app-wide for rule-violation only.
Output: Extended union/table + ring prop in HexCell; migrated HexCell tests; HexGrid.test.tsx `SAFE_FILL`/`goalFill` constants updated to the new values.
</objective>
