<objective>
Extend the single-source highlight table in `HexCell.tsx` to cover every real hex-tint case and re-map the traffic-light semantics (HILITE-01/02, D-01/D-02/D-03). This is the contract plan that 33-06 (HexGrid consolidation) depends on: it adds the `gk-kick-target`, `pass-target`, and `tackle-risk` tint types, recolors `safe` gold→green and `goal` red→purple, and adds an independent `ring?: 'required' | 'confirmed'` overlay prop (mirroring `PieceOverlay`'s `isOffside`/`isMovedThisStage` pattern) for the compound centre-hex and confirmed-pass rings. It exports both `HIGHLIGHT_STYLES` and a new `RING_STYLES` table as the single source of truth, and migrates the affected `HexCell.test.tsx` and the `HexGrid.test.tsx` color constants to assert against those imported tables (not retyped literals) so the suite stays green after the recolor.

Purpose: One lookup table becomes the sole owner of every mutually-exclusive hex tint, with red reserved app-wide for rule-violation only.
Output: Extended + exported `HIGHLIGHT_STYLES` and a new exported `RING_STYLES` table + ring prop in HexCell; HexCell tests asserting against those tables (not retyped literals); HexGrid.test.tsx `SAFE_FILL`/`goalFill`/`KICKOFF_FILL`/risk constants derived from the imported `HIGHLIGHT_STYLES` table.
</objective>
