<objective>
Fix the two REPLAY-06 server-side ball-tracking root causes diagnosed in 18.1-RESEARCH.md so post-game replay never shows the ball at a stale or skipped position.

Purpose: Live-session/post-game replay must track ball position correctly on every frame including pickups, intercepted passes, steals, header deliveries, and GK punts (REPLAY-06). Two concrete defects exist: (1) a successful steal/tackle during movement records the MOVE event's `ballAfter` from a pre-contest snapshot, producing a one-frame ball glitch; (2) `HEADED_PASS` and `GK_PUNT` (added by quick-task 260621-b8f after Phase 14's replay migration) carry a correct `ballAfter` but were never added to `REPLAY_ELIGIBLE_TYPES`, so their ball movement produces no visible replay frame at all (ball appears to teleport).

Output: Edits to `packages/server/src/gameEngine.ts` (REPLAY_ELIGIBLE_TYPES + applyMove ballAfter) and new regression cases in `packages/server/src/__tests__/replay.integration.test.ts`.
</objective>
