<objective>
Fix DESIGN-02: stop HexGrid from doing wasted per-frame interactive derivation during post-game replay.

Purpose: Post-game replay playback must produce no unnecessary re-render work (DESIGN-02). 18.1-RESEARCH.md traced the only real cost center to `HexGrid.tsx`, which unconditionally runs its full ~962-hex highlight/click-handler derivation and its 22-piece selectability cascade on every received `GAME_STATE` frame — including all replay frames — even though `phase === 'REPLAY'` makes every derived interactive boolean inert (nothing is clickable, no highlight is meaningful). Server emission is already clean (exactly one emit per 500ms tick) and `setGameState` is NOT the bottleneck (its expensive branches are gated behind `prevSelectedId !== null`, always null during replay) — so no server or store change is in scope.

Output: A `phase === 'REPLAY'` guard added to `HexGrid.tsx` (highlight-set derivation skipped, `onClick` forced undefined, `isClickable` cascade forced false) plus REPLAY-phase assertions in `HexGrid.test.tsx`. Pieces, ball, and base hex fill continue to render so replay stays visually correct.
</objective>
