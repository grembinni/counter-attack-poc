<objective>
Close UAT gap 6: the attacking manager must explicitly select AND confirm a penalty taker, exactly like the corner-kick and offside free-kick taker steps — and, once the taker is placed, must have a real Shoot control to take the kick.

Purpose: today a single board click on any own non-goalkeeper piece fires `emitPenaltyKickTaker` immediately with no confirmation step, so a misclick commits the taker irreversibly (`PENALTY_KICK_TAKER_PLACED` is an Undo boundary). Separately, the `PENALTY_KICK` phase renders a panel with the text "Take your penalty kick." and no button at all — the `GAME_ROLL` penalty branch exists server-side but nothing in the UI reaches it.

Output: a selection-only store branch, a Confirm button on the panel, and a shoot-only CTA for the kick itself.
</objective>
