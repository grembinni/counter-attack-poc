<objective>
Close UAT gap 5: any foul inside the penalty area awards a penalty kick, and the award performs the full setup the user specified — goalkeeper on the goal-line centre, the box cleared of everyone else, kicker chosen before repositioning, and repositioning that cannot move the kicker or goalkeeper and cannot enter the box.

Purpose: `applyFoulChoice` currently routes to `triggerPenaltyKick` only when `foulSource === 'GK_DIVE_AT_FEET'`, so a tackle in the six-yard box awards a free kick. And `triggerPenaltyKick` performs none of the award-time board setup — it just moves the ball to the spot and opens the attacking reposition window with the kicker still unknown, which is also why "the kicker cannot be moved" is unenforceable today.

Output: a box-location trigger, an award-time board setup, a reordered phase chain, and reposition guards — all server-side. The kicker-selection UX itself lands in Plan 39-23.

## Coordinate contract (read before implementing)

- `PENALTY_SPOT` (`packages/shared/src/pitch.ts` lines 103-106) is keyed by the **DEFENDING** team: `home: {q:4,r:13}` is the spot inside the HOME box, used when AWAY is kicking. The existing `triggerPenaltyKick` line `const spot = PENALTY_SPOT[defendingTeam]` is therefore already correct. 39-UAT test 5's `expected:` line pairs the two spots the other way round ("(32,13) for away kicking"); that is a transcription slip in the UAT write-up, and the UAT's own `## Gaps` entry confirms `PENALTY_SPOT` "already matches the user's stated kick-spot coordinates exactly". Do NOT swap the keying.
- Home defends `q = 0` (`homeGoal`, `homePenaltyArea` at `q <= 5`); away defends `q = 36` (`awayGoal`, `awayPenaltyArea` at `q >= 31`). The user's "keeper automatically moves to the center goalline 36,13 / 0,13" therefore means away → `{q:36,r:13}` and home → `{q:0,r:13}`.
