<objective>
Close UAT gaps 1, 2 and 9: a foul that came out of a SUCCESSFUL duel must offer only a restart (there is no play left to continue — possession already changed hands); the restart must be placed where the BALL was, not where the fouling defender stopped; and each of corner kick, free kick and penalty kick must charge 1 minute of game clock like the goal kick already does.

Purpose: all three are wrong-outcome defects in the already-shipped Phase 39 foul chain, observed in the live two-browser UAT session. Gap 1 and gap 2 both live in `applyMove`'s duel branches and `applyFoulChoice`, so they are fixed together to avoid two plans rewriting the same twenty lines. Gap 9 is a three-site clock charge that shares no function with them.

Output: a `foulDuelSucceeded` flag on the foul state cluster with a server-side rejection and a matching client-side button suppression, a carrier-sourced `foulHex`, a `+1` minute charge on the three restart resolutions, and regression coverage for each.
</objective>
