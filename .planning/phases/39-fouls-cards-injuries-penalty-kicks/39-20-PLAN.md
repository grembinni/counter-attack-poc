<objective>
Close UAT gaps 3 (server half) and 4: make the dive-at-feet a two-step action — accept, then choose the destination hex — and actually move the goalkeeper's piece to that hex; and close the hole in the shared once-per-movement-cycle dive cap that let a keeper still block a snapshot after diving.

Purpose: today `applyGkDiveAtFeetResponse` resolves the whole duel on the accept click with a destination the manager never picked, and teleports the goalkeeper onto the carrier's hex only on SUCCESS. The user requires the manager to choose an in-range hex next to the carrier and the goalkeeper's piece to move there as part of the action. Separately, `gameHandlers.ts`'s snapshot resolution sets `phase: 'GK_DIVE'` directly rather than through `enterGkDiveOrSkip`, which is why the observed keeper could still dive to save inside the same movement phase.

Output: a new `GK_DIVE_AT_FEET_TARGET` phase with a shared legal-target helper, server-side target validation and goalkeeper relocation, and a fifth GK_DIVE entry point brought under the D-09 cap. The client interaction for choosing that hex lands in Plan 39-21.
</objective>
