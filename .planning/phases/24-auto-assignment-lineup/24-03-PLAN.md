confirm. Instead, compute each team's auto-assignment, store it on the room, and emit
`LINEUP_ASSIGNMENT_READY` privately to each socket. Add `LINEUP_SWAP` (validated outfield swap,
GK-locked, per-socket update) and `LINEUP_CONFIRM` (parallel both-players gate that finally calls
`buildInitialGameState` and broadcasts `GAME_STATE`).

formation-confirm through to kick-off.
