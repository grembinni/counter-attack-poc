## Tests

### 1. BUG-19 — Jersey numbers correct on pieces

expected: Piece overlays on the board show the correct CSV-seeded jersey number for each player (e.g. the striker shows "9", the goalkeeper shows "1"). Numbers match the player's actual squad number, not a derived value from their piece ID.
result: pass

### 2. BUG-15 — Goal log entries use standard gray (no amber)

expected: When a goal is scored, the Action Log entry for it appears in standard gray text — the same colour as all other entries. No amber/yellow highlight on GOAL or SHOT_ATTEMPT log lines.
result: pass

### 3. BUG-16 — FTP/HP move entries show full player names

expected: Action Log entries for HP_MOVE and FTP_MOVE show the player's full name and jersey number (e.g. "J. Smith #7 moves to D6"), matching the format used by other move-type events. Not just "A" or "D" prefix.
result: pass

### 4. BUG-10 — Clicking a spent piece opens player card

expected: In the MOVE phase, after a piece has already moved (spent), clicking that piece opens its player card / inspect panel on the right. It does NOT re-highlight move targets. Spent pieces on the other team are unaffected (still unclickable).
result: pass

### 5. BUG-21 — SNAPSHOT_TARGET goal-line hexes highlight for attacker

expected: During SNAPSHOT_TARGET, the goal-line hexes are visibly tinted/highlighted on the attacking player's board so they can select where to aim. (Previously live-verified during execution — confirm still works.)
result: pass

### 6. BUG-13 — Multi-defender tackle: all adjacent defenders get attempt

expected: When a moving defender fails a tackle on the ball carrier, any other defender already standing adjacent to the carrier also receives a tackle attempt in the same move resolution. Possession transfers immediately if any secondary tackle succeeds. Only one dice injection set per move call.
result: pass

### 7. BUG-20 — FREE_MOVE offer not shown mid-MOVE-slot or mid-HEADER

expected: If the ball crosses a zone boundary during a MOVE or HEADER sub-phase, no FREE_MOVE_ATTACK/FREE_MOVE_DEFENSE offer pops up mid-sequence. The free-move interrupt fires at the next clean boundary (after a PASS or after the HEADER resolves), not during an active movement slot.
result: pass

### 8. BUG-06 — Offside cleared after kick-off restart following a goal

expected: After a goal is scored and the game restarts with a kick-off, no pieces are highlighted as offside from the previous phase of play. The pitch is clean — offside indicators from before the goal do not persist into the new kick-off setup.
result: pass

### 9. BUG-07 — Header duel winner delivers ball directly

expected: When a header duel is won, the winning player's current hex is used as the delivery target automatically — there is no extra step asking the winner to select a target hex (GAME_HEADER_TARGET). The ball moves immediately to the correct destination (GK dive or pass).
result: pass

### 10. BUG-12 — First-time pass delivers directly (no reposition sub-phase)

expected: After a successful first-time pass roll, the ball is delivered directly to the target hex. No FIRST_TIME_PASS_MOVE repositioning sub-phase appears asking the passer to reposition. Delivery follows the same occupant-pickup rules as a standard pass.
result: pass

### 11. BUG-14 — Snapshot available after carrier's pace is exhausted

expected: If the ball carrier uses all their movement pace in a MOVE phase (pace exhausted), the Snapshot option is still available to them — they are not locked out of taking a snapshot due to pace exhaustion. The Snapshot button / action remains selectable.
result: pass

### 12. BUG-17 — Kick-off setup piece repositions visible in replay

expected: When replaying a kick-off setup sequence, each piece reposition during KICK_OFF_SETUP appears in the replay frames — pieces move to their new positions step by step in the replay, rather than all appearing at once or not at all.
result: pass

### 13. BUG-18 — Undo works across all covered movement phases

expected: The Undo button is functional in these phases: MOVE, GK_KICK_MOVE, SNAPSHOT_DEFLECT, FREE_KICK_SETUP, and FREE_MOVE. Pressing Undo in any of these phases reverses the last piece move. Undo is NOT available during KICK_OFF_SETUP (intentionally excluded).
result: pass
