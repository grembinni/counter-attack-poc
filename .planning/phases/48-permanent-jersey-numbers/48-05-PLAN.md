<objective>
Close the `DRAFT_REARRANGE` bench-number orphan gap: a card moved from a lineup slot onto the bench after `draftComplete` currently has no entry in `DraftSession.*BenchNumbers`, and the read site's `?? 0` fallback silently gives that player jersey number `0`.

Purpose: 48-RESEARCH.md Critical Finding 3. Bench numbers are drawn exactly once at the `draftComplete` transition, but rearranging remains legal afterwards until the requesting side confirms (the `T-29-07-01` lifecycle guard) — and `applyRearrange` moves ids between `lineupSlots` and `benchIds` without ever touching the number maps.

**Resolution of 48-RESEARCH.md Open Question 1 (eager vs. lazy backfill): EAGER, at `DRAFT_REARRANGE`.** Research asked for a confirming grep of the client for any pre-confirm bench-number display. That grep found one: `packages/client/src/components/LineupAssignmentScreen.tsx:1332` passes `benchNumbers={draftView.benchNumbers}` to `BenchCarousel` inside the draft branch, which renders continuously — including in the post-`draftComplete`, pre-`LINEUP_CONFIRM` window where rearranging happens. A lazy backfill at `LINEUP_CONFIRM` would leave a just-rearranged bench card rendering with no number (`BenchCarousel.tsx:240` omits the `#n` span when the id is missing) until the user clicks Confirm. Eager backfill keeps `*BenchNumbers` in lockstep with `*BenchIds` and makes the already-emitted `DRAFT_STATE_UPDATED` unicast carry the right number immediately.
Output: A `backfillBenchNumbers` call in the `DRAFT_REARRANGE` handler, an idempotent draft-complete assignment, and an integration regression test.
</objective>
