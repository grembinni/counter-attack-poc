<objective>
Make `applyRosterReposition` stop re-binding `PlayerPiece.number` to the formation slot so a repositioned player carries their own jersey number with them, and lock that behavior in through every goal/half-time reset path.

Purpose: This is the single most-visible violation of NUMBER-01/NUMBER-02 — the function currently documents "number stays bound to the slot" as deliberate design (a Phase 42 decision that Phase 48 explicitly supersedes per 48-CONTEXT.md D-05). Once this lands, `applyRosterContinuity` (already correct — it spreads the whole live piece and overrides only `position`) automatically satisfies NUMBER-04 at all four reset call sites with zero further change.
Output: A two-line deletion in the piece-build block, an inverted docstring, a recorded decision on the `ROSTER_REPOSITION` event's jersey-number fields, and a rewritten test file that asserts the new contract instead of the old one.
</objective>
