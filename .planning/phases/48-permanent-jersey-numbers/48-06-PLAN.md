Replace the kick-off striker anchor's `p.number === 9` lookup with a formation-slot lookup (`slotId === 'ST'`), so the anchor depends on the tactical position a manager assigned rather than on a jersey number that this phase has just made mutable and person-owned.

Output: A slot-index anchor in `buildSquadPieces`, an updated diagnostic message, refreshed docstrings, and slot-keyed test coverage extended to all four formations and both attacking sides.
