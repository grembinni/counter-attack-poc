**UX-10 (FREE_MOVE_ATTACK / FREE_MOVE_DEFENSE):** Replaced the old two-part helper (line-1 `"Free Move!"` + line-2 `"{sideLabel} — move up to 6 hexes ... {remaining} of {eligibleTotal} players left to move."`) with the locked UX-10 copy:

- `'active player sees the Free Move helper text (attacking team)'` — matcher updated to `/reposition up to 6 hexes regardless of remaining pace/i`
- `'active player sees the Free Move helper text (defending team)'` — same
- `'FREE_MOVE_ATTACK: 3 eligible, 1 moved — helper reads 2 players left to move'` — test name + matcher updated to `"2 players still eligible to move"`

### Task 2 — UX-13 Action Button Tooltips

Added module-level `ACTION_SUMMARY: Record<string, string>` with 11 entries:

```
Move → "Move a player; distance limited by pace."
Standard Pass → "Pass to a nearby teammate; may be intercepted."
One-Touch → "First-time pass; both teams reposition before it lands."
High Pass → "Lofted pass contested by an aerial header."
Long Ball → "A long downfield pass; less accurate."
Snapshot → "A quick shot from inside the box."
Shoot → "Take a shot at goal (in range only)."
Punt (High Pass) → "Goalkeeper clears with a long kick."
Quick Throw → "Goalkeeper throws the ball back into play."
Undo → "Undo your last move this phase."
End Turn → "End your turn and pass control to the opponent."
```

Applied `title={ACTION_SUMMARY['...']}` on all 21 `ctaButton` elements across every phase branch:

- HIGH_PASS_MOVE: Undo, End Turn
- FIRST_TIME_PASS_MOVE: Undo, End Turn
- SNAPSHOT_DEFLECT: End Turn
- GK_RESTART: Punt (High Pass), Quick Throw, Move
- GK_KICK_MOVE: End Turn
- FREE_MOVE: End Turn
- PASS/KICK_OFF loose-ball: Move
- PASS action chooser: Move, Standard Pass, One-Touch, High Pass, Long Ball, Snapshot, Shoot
- MOVE phase: Snapshot, Undo, End Turn

No new npm dependency, no new tooltip component, no CSS changes — native browser title attribute per 18-PATTERNS.md `§Native title tooltip` precedent confirmed at `KickOffSetupPanel.tsx:115`, `FreeKickSetupPanel.tsx:137`, `GameBoard.tsx:234,333`.
