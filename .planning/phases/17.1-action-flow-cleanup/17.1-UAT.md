## Tests

### 1. Aerial Ability stat label

expected: Open the Player Stats panel for any player (goalkeeper or outfielder). The aerial stat is labeled "Aerial Ability" — there is no separate "Heading" stat anywhere.
result: pass

### 2. Tackle and steal ZoI are independent per action type

expected: When a defender attempts a tackle against the ball carrier and fails, that same defender no longer shows a tackle-risk (orange) zone for the rest of the sequence — the carrier can move past them again without re-triggering a tackle from that defender. If the carrier instead tries to dribble directly into that defender's hex (steal), the steal risk is still shown and still active.
result: issue
reported: "After a failed tackle the threat no longer show but neither does valid move squares next to the ball carrier. After a failed tackle the defender should still be able to move next to the ball carrier it just wont trigger a new tackle this move phase. After a failed steal the defender threat hexes still shows. After a failed steal the attacker should still be able to move next to the defender it just wont trigger a new steal from that defender this move phase. Threat hexes should show save movement if there are no defenders in range who can still make a steal or tackle action (depending on the use case)"
severity: major

### 3. First-time pass repositioning phase

expected: After completing an accurate first-time pass, both teams get a turn to reposition one player up to 1 hex (you'll see "First-time pass! Move 1 player to receive the ball." with Undo/End Turn controls). After both teams end their turn, the ball is delivered to the declared target hex and play resumes normally.
result: issue
reported: "there was no prompt for either team to move after first-time pass"
severity: major

### 4. GK Restart when keeper ends a move holding the ball in their own box

expected: If your goalkeeper is the ball carrier and ends the MOVE phase positioned inside their own penalty area, the game transitions to a GK Restart phase (kick/throw/move options) instead of the normal choose-action state.
result: pass

### 5. GK save spill keeps the ball with the keeper

expected: On a shot save where the goalkeeper doesn't cleanly hold the ball (a "spill"), the keeper still ends up holding the ball and the game transitions to GK Restart — the ball does not become a loose ball out on the pitch.
result: pass

### 6. Loose ball never scatters off the pitch

expected: When the ball becomes loose near the edge of the pitch and the scatter roll would send it past the boundary, the ball stops at the last hex still on the pitch instead of leaving the playing area.
result: issue
reported: "yes but a bug with loose ball - [LOOSE BALL] 21,4 -> 24,1 - that movement is not possible with 1 roll for direction and 1 roll for range will need to review all loose ball behavior and make sure it aligns with 1 roll for direction and 1 roll for range"
severity: major

### 7. Regular shot beyond 11 hexes is rejected

expected: Trying to declare a regular shot at a goal-line hex more than 11 hexes from the shooter is rejected — that hex isn't a valid shot target.
result: issue
reported: "not working as expected, i.e. when attacker is at 11,5 - goal hexes 0,10->0,12 are the only hexes in range and should be the only shot targets highlighted. Currently all goal line hexes are highlighted"
severity: major

### 8. Passing directly to a hex occupied by a defender is now allowed

expected: A standard pass aimed at a hex where an opposing defender is standing is now a legal pass target (no longer blocked outright). The ball goes to that hex and the defender standing there immediately intercepts it — no dice roll needed.
result: pass
