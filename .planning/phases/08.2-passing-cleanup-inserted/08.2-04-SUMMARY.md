## Decisions Made

- MISSING_TARGET (not INVALID_TARGET) emitted when targetHex is entirely absent from the payload — keeps error semantics distinct from shape validation failures
- passTypeMap object inline in handler body instead of a shared constant — the mapping is only needed here and avoids a shared module coupling
- HEADER confirm guard placed after the HEADER sequence check (ELIGIBLE_NEXT_ACTIONS) to maintain guard ordering consistency with existing SHOT/HEADER sequence checks
