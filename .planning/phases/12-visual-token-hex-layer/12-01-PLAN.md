<objective>
Refactor PieceOverlay to (a) render team-distinguishing stripe patterns on outfield tokens via per-piece SVG `<defs><pattern>`, and (b) replace the four boolean selection props (`isSelected`, `isClickable`, `isSpent`, `isHeaderContestant`) with a single `selectionState: SelectionState` enum that drives three distinct outline states.

Purpose: VIS-01 requires home/away tokens to be instantly distinguishable by stripe (not just fill color). UX-05 requires three simultaneous, visually distinct selection-outline states (selectable/active/activated). This plan delivers both at the leaf component so HexGrid (Plan 04) can consume the new API.

Output: Updated `PieceOverlay.tsx` exporting a `SelectionState` type, plus a new `PieceOverlay.test.tsx` covering both requirements.
</objective>
