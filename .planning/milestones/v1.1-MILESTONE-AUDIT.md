## Critical Gaps

### 1. REPLAY-06 — Ball Tracking Bugs Deferred

**REQ:** Ball position updates correctly on every replay frame.

**Finding:** Plan 14-02 implemented `ballAfter` universally in `buildReplayFrames` with a passing unit test. However, UAT test 6 (`14-UAT.md`) confirmed ball tracking bugs in a live session and explicitly deferred them:

> "there are some bugs but defer fixing them to a later phase" — severity: minor

**Status:** The unit test verifies `ballAfter` propagation in `buildReplayFrames`, but the UAT identified live-session edge cases not covered by the test. REPLAY-06 is NOT fully satisfied.

**Files:** `packages/server/src/gameEngine.ts` (buildReplayFrames), `.planning/phases/14-kick-off-rules-replay/14-UAT.md`

### 2. MATCH-06 — Requirement Text Conflict

**REQ:** "Default kick off setup places midfielders and backs within hex columns 6–20"

**Finding:** After implementation and UAT, Away DEF/MID positions were restored to symmetric mirror positions:

- Away DEF: q=30 (mirrors Home DEF q=6 on 37-col board: 36-30=6)
- Away MID: q=26 (mirrors Home MID q=10: 36-26=10)

Commit `6404725` message: _"MATCH-06 incorrectly pushed Away DEF to q=20 and Away MID to q=16, placing them too close to centre."_

These positions do NOT satisfy q∈[6,20] literally, but they DO correctly mirror the home formation symmetrically. The requirement text was written from Home perspective only.

**Status:** Implementation is correct by design. Requirement text needs updating to match intent.

**Resolution options:**

- A. Update requirement to "each team's DEF/MID start within 12 hexes of the kick-off hex" (captures symmetric intent)
- B. Revert to literal q∈[6,20] for both teams (Away DEF at q=19, Away MID at q=16) — UAT showed this was wrong
