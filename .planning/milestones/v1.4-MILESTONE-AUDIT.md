One genuine integration blocker was found, unrelated to the phases' own internal correctness:

**`createServer.ts:99-167` reconnect handling** — a misplaced `return` (line 166) sits inside the outer session-token check but outside the inner `room.status === 'playing'` check. Any reconnecting socket whose room isn't currently `'playing'` (mid-draft, or room gone) returns early and never falls through to fresh-connection handler registration — that socket silently ends up with **no handlers registered at all**. This is a pre-existing Phase 07 defect that Phase 29's own verification flagged as out-of-scope-but-unremediated (since Phase 29 was, until now, the last phase in the roadmap). No phase in this milestone fixed it, and it remains present today.
