<objective>
Fix the loose-ball path origin on a blocked shot (BUG-36 / D-14). When the shooter-vs-GK duel
resolves as a TIE, `applyRoll`'s SHOT case currently leaves the ball at `state.ball.position` —
the shooter's own hex — so the ensuing LOOSE_BALL scatter walks outward from the shooter instead
of from the keeper who blocked it.

Purpose: this is the defect folded in from
`.planning/todos/pending/loose-ball-pathing-blocked-shot-wrong-origin.md`.

**Root cause was CORRECTED during research — read this before writing any code.** Both
`36-CONTEXT.md` (D-14) and the folded todo point at `computeShotPathDeflection` (~line 3615) and
the `computeLooseBall` scatter walk (~lines 2757-2766). RESEARCH.md verified against current
source that **both of those are already correct** and must NOT be touched. The bug lives 445 lines
away, in the SHOT case's `outcome === 'LOOSE_BALL'` tie branch: the `ball` assignment at ~line 2310
and the `shotAttempt` event's `ballAfter` at ~line 2302. The correct value, `gkEffectivePos`, is
already computed at ~line 2111 and is already used correctly by the sibling SAVE branches.

Output: a two-field correction in the tie branch plus regression tests that specifically construct
a GK-duel tie (not an outfield deflection) and assert the scatter origin end-to-end.
</objective>
