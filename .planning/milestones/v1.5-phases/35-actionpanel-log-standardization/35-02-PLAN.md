<objective>
Bring `ActionLog.tsx` and `ActionLog.module.css` to one consistent language and format
system: remove the duplicated internal panel header (D-10), remove the container border
(D-01), standardize goalkeeper terminology to "Keeper" (D-03), formalize the ✓/✗ glyph rule
(D-04), convert all ALL-CAPS narration to sentence case (D-11), and replace every ASCII
`-> ` arrow with the unicode `→` (D-12) — while explicitly preserving raw axial hex
coordinates (D-13).

Purpose: PANEL-02 and PANEL-04 for the ActionLog half of the D-05 component set. The log is
the single densest source of language drift in the app: two stacked headers say the same
thing, three arrow styles coexist, and outcome narration mixes shouted enum values
(`SUCCESS`, `ACCURATE`, `SCORED!`) with sentence-case prose (`Inaccurate — loose ball`) for
the same event type.

Output: a `formatEvent` switch where every case follows one glyph rule, one arrow glyph, and
one casing convention, plus a border-free single-headed log panel.
</objective>
