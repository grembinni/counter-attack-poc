<objective>
Close the six-hop plumbing chain: carry `refereeLeniencyOverride` and `refereeLeniencyValue` from the client payload plan 44-03 now produces, across the socket contract, through server-side allow-list validation, onto `Room`, and into the `buildInitialGameState` parameters plan 44-01 added.

Purpose: REFEREE-01 and REFEREE-02 only become real behaviour once the host's choice actually reaches the engine; REFEREE-04's coupling then holds automatically because `refereeCard.leniency` is the single field both consumers read. This plan also introduces the phase's only new untrusted-input surface, so its allow-list guards are the phase's primary security deliverable.

This is the plan where the two new payload fields become **required** on the wire contract. That intentionally breaks compilation at all 34 existing `ROOM_SETTINGS_CONFIRM` fixture call sites across 8 test files — Task 3 repairs them. Required-not-optional is the correct choice: the sibling `tackleStealDecline` field is required, and making these optional would let a legitimate client omit them and then be rejected at runtime by the very guards Task 2 adds.

Output: the wire contract, `Room` fields, two ASVS guards, both broadcast sites, the engine call site, and a fully green monorepo.
</objective>
