<objective>
Close gap item 7 from `42-10-SUMMARY.md`: a defending player is not auto-moved to the minimum legal distance from a free kick / foul kick (the defensive wall distance rule).

**Scope note — this item is explicitly OUT of Phase 42's chartered scope.** It is a pre-existing defect from the Phase 17/25 `FREE_KICK_SETUP` staged rework (OFFSIDE-02, D-30/D-49/D-50), discovered incidentally during Phase 42's live two-browser playtest. The human verifier asked for it to be carried in this phase's gap-closure round rather than filed separately (`42-10-SUMMARY.md` § "Additional out-of-phase-scope bug"). It shares no requirement, no component, and no user-facing surface with gap items 1-6.

Purpose: today the wall rule is enforced by AUTO-MOVE at exactly one moment (`relocateTrappedFreeKickPieces`, called only when the free kick is first awarded) and by BLOCKING REJECTION at every other moment (`applyFreeKickReady`'s `DEFENDER_TOO_CLOSE`, returned at the end of each defending stage, index 1 and 3). The rejection reason has **no client-side message mapping anywhere in `packages/client`** — grep confirms zero references — so a defending manager whose piece is inside the bubble clicks Ready and simply nothing happens. That silent dead-end is what the verifier experienced as "the defender is not auto-moved".

Output: the wall rule becomes an invariant the engine maintains automatically at every defending-stage boundary, with the silent-rejection path removed.
</objective>

## Domain rules this plan depends on (verified in the current tree)

- `FREE_KICK_STAGES` (`packages/shared/src/offside.ts:38`) = `[{kicking,4},{defending,4},{kicking,3},{defending,2}]`. Defending stages are indices **1 and 3**.
- Minimum legal defender distance is **> 2 hexes**, i.e. `hexDistance(piece.position, freeKickHex) >= 3`. Enforced today at `gameEngine.ts:9542` (`<= 2` → `DEFENDER_TOO_CLOSE`) and mirrored in `applyFreeKickMove`'s destination check.
- `relocateTrappedFreeKickPieces(state, team?)` (`gameEngine.ts:9132`) already implements the exact auto-move the verifier expects: every ACTIVE piece of `team` within 2 hexes of `freeKickHex` is moved to an unoccupied ring-3 hex, preferring the 4 ring-3 hexes closest to that piece's own goal line, with a random `>= 3` fallback. It is `isActivePiece`-aware (BUG-38) and only ever rewrites `position` — it never touches `freeKickPlacedPieceIds`, `movedPieceIds`, or any counter.
- It is currently called from exactly two places: `applyOffsideFoulWithRelocation` (`:9090`) and `applyFoulChoice`'s restart branch (`:1780` conceding team, `:1791` kicking team). Neither runs again after the free kick is awarded.
