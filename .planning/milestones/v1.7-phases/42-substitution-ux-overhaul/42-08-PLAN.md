<objective>
Replace substitution mode's fire-on-drop behavior with a staged, confirm-before-apply flow: one pending swap at a time, a popup naming both players, and a return to positioning mode on confirm.

Purpose: a substitution consumes a capped 3-per-team resource with no undo (REQUIREMENTS.md lists undo/redo for a confirmed substitution as explicitly out of scope), so it is the one irreversible action this phase adds and needs a confirmation gate. A position swap does not, which is why D-02 keeps repositioning instant. The app already has an established confirm-before-commit pattern in `ActionPanel.tsx`'s `withEndTurnConfirm`/`confirmDialog` pair, and SUB-14 says to mirror it rather than invent a new one.

Output: a staged `pendingSub` with a confirmation popup wired to the existing `onSubstitute` prop, with SUB-15's asymmetric confirm/cancel outcomes.
</objective>
