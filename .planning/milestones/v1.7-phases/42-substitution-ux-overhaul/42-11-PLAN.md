## Gap-closure decision recorded

`42-UI-SPEC.md` and `BenchCarousel.test.tsx` currently lock a "UI-SPEC coexistence" rule (glyph AND text badge both render on a red-carded bench card). **That lock is superseded by the live human verification recorded in `42-10-SUMMARY.md` § "Section D — Red card / BUG-38"**, which reports the coexistence as a defect and requires a single indicator. The surviving indicator is the `RED CARD` text badge (`data-testid="bench-red-card-badge"`), not the glyph, because:

1. The text badge is unambiguous ("RED CARD") where the 16px rectangle glyph is what the verifier described as a "standard/plain card icon".
2. The bench already uses a text-badge vocabulary for its other status (`OUT`), so keeping the badge keeps the bench internally consistent.
3. It is the narrower change: the glyph is suppressed only for `redCarded === true` bench cards, leaving yellow-card and injury rendering, and all three non-bench surfaces, completely untouched.
