# Milestone v1.3: Team Customization & Formation System

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-07-11
**Phases:** 19–25
**Total Plans:** 27

## Overview

Expanded from 4 fictional teams to 12 real-league teams (6 MLS + 6 International) with full visual identities; added a parameterized 18-style uniform selection system; implemented 4 dynamic formations (4-4-2, 5-3-2, 4-3-3, 3-4-3) with stat-driven auto-assignment and player swap; closed OFFSIDE-01/02 human UAT with a redesigned FREE_KICK_SETUP step sequence; fixed GK_KICK and LOOSE_BALL_LAND replay visibility; fixed jersey centering and Style 12 SVG pattern. Decoupled team/player data to lay foundations for v1.4 draft mode.
